Frequently asked questions

Can I book a table?

You don't need to book: you can just walk in. Coming with a larger group, or want to be sure there's enough focaccia? Just send us a message and we'll make sure it's ready for you.

Can I eat in?

Yes, there's a cosy coffee corner. Enjoy your focaccia with an Italian espresso or moka coffee, or take it all to go.

Is there a vegetarian or vegan choice?

Yes. Several filled focaccias are vegetarian, and the Firenze and all the classic focaccias are vegan too.

How can I pay?

By card or cash, in the shop. Online orders are also simply settled at pickup.

Where can I park?

We're in the centre, so you park in one of the nearby car parks. Coming by bike or on foot is easiest.

Do you do catering too?

Yes, from Focaccia Boxes to bespoke catering for drinks receptions, parties and business gatherings. Check out the catering page.
