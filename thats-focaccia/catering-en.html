Frequently asked questions about catering

What does catering cost?

That depends on the number of guests and what you choose, which is why we work with a proposal made for you. Your request is always without obligation, and the prices of the individual focaccias on the menu already give you a fair idea.

How many people can I order for?

From a small drinks reception to a larger event: tell us the number of guests and we'll think along about the right quantities, combinations and presentation.

Is there a vegetarian or vegan option too?

Certainly. Several filled focaccias are vegetarian, and the Firenze and all the classic focaccias are vegan too. We're happy to tailor the box to your group.
