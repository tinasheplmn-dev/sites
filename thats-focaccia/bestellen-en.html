Good to know

Do I have to order online, or can I just walk in?

Walking in is always fine, Wed to Sun from 10:00 to 15:30. Ordering online is an extra option: your order is then ready at the time you choose, with no waiting.

How do I pay?

At pickup in the shop, by card or cash. You don't pay anything online.

How quickly is my order ready?

Choose "as soon as possible" and everything is ready for you after about 20 minutes. Choosing a later time that day or on another day works too.
