How does ordering online work?

Tap + on anything on the menu, choose collection or delivery and a time, and leave your name and number. No account and no app fees. You pay when you collect or when your food arrives.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Choose delivery when you order. We call you to confirm your address is in our delivery area, and any delivery charge, before we cook. You pay on delivery.

How late are you open?

Monday to Wednesday until 00:30, Thursday to Saturday until 02:00, and Sunday until 23:00. Every day from 11:00.

Is the food halal?

Yes. Real Taste is a halal fast food shop.

Can I sit in?

Yes, there is plenty of space to sit down and eat. You can also walk in and order at the counter.

Can you do a big order for the office or a party?

Yes, from 10 boxes. Send a team or party request and we call you to confirm what goes in the boxes and the price.

I have an allergy. Can you help?

Yes. Call us on 07378 251430 before you order and we tell you what goes into each dish.
