About La Calle

The story behind La Calle.

A new driving school in Eindhoven, with its own view on learning to drive.

The name

La Calle

La Calle is Spanish for the street. To us the name stands for freedom, direction and the choices you learn to make yourself along the way. Driving is not a trick you memorise, but a skill you grow into.

You see it in how we work: reflection after every lesson, honest expectations about your progress and lessons built around you.

Freedom
Direction
Your own choices

Diego, your instructor

At La Calle you have one instructor: Diego. With over three years of teaching experience he has guided starters, switchers and internationals. Every lesson he knows where you stood last week and what you need today.

What students write about him most: he stays calm, explains until you truly get it and is honest about where you stand. In English or in Dutch.

The car

You learn to drive a manual in our Renault Clio: easy to oversee, a smooth clutch and comfortable for all exam manoeuvres.

At home in Eindhoven

We know the teaching area inside out: the tricky crossings, the exam routes and the best places to practise.
