Terms and conditions

The agreements in writing.

Clear and without fine print. These are the terms that apply to all lessons, packages and exams at driving school La Calle. The Dutch version is legally binding.

Article 1. What driving school La Calle commits to

  1. 1.1

    The student receives theory and/or practical lessons from an instructor who meets the requirements of the Dutch WRM act (Wet Rijonderricht Motorrijtuigen).

  2. 1.2

    The driving exam is booked once the exam costs or the agreed package have been paid in full. A lesson lasts 45 minutes. We advise students to drive at least 2 lessons (90 minutes) per session.

  3. 1.3

    If lessons or an exam cannot take place, the student is informed as soon as possible and a new appointment is made immediately, or another instructor is arranged where possible. In such cases the student cannot claim any refund or compensation.

  4. 1.4

    One 45-minute lesson from the package is used for the practical exam itself. The student decides how many lessons to drive before the exam, with a minimum of 1 lesson. Our advice is 2 lessons.

  5. 1.5

    The top-up package is only valid after purchasing another package, or if you switched from another driving school after taking your exam.

Article 2. What the student commits to

  1. 2.1

    To take lessons the student must be at least 16.5 years old and always carry a valid ID during lessons.

  2. 2.2

    The student keeps to the agreed date, time and place for the lesson. The school applies a maximum waiting time of 10 minutes, which is deducted from the lesson time. In case of a no-show without timely cancellation (24 hours in advance), the full lesson is charged because of the reserved time.

  3. 2.3

    A lesson cancelled too late is not charged in case of an urgent reason, such as a funeral of a close family member (up to the second degree), emergency hospital admission or own illness, demonstrated with a doctor's note. Written proof is required.
