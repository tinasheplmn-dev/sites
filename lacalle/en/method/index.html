Our method

How you learn to drive at La Calle.

A clear route in four phases, at your own pace. Every phase builds on the previous one.

The debrief after every lesson

Engine off, a short debrief. What went well, where did you hesitate, what is the focus next time? Together you set the goal for the next lesson. Students mention this moment in their reviews as especially valuable.

01

The first metres

On quiet roads you get to know the car: clutch, gears, observation and steering. You build the foundation, without any rush.

What you learn here

  • Vehicle control
  • Observation technique
  • Pulling away and stopping
02

Into real traffic

Crossings, roundabouts and right of way. Diego explains step by step and skips nothing, until you read the situations yourself.

What you learn here

  • Crossings and roundabouts
  • Right of way
  • Observe, decide, act
03

Driving independently

You choose the route and make the decisions. Diego only steps in when needed and discusses why afterwards.

What you learn here

  • Independent route driving
  • Motorway and rush hour
  • Handling surprises
04

Ready for the exam

Exam routes, special manoeuvres and a calm mind. If nerves get the better of you, an anxiety exam is possible.

What you learn here

  • Exam routes in the region
  • Special manoeuvres
  • Anxiety exam possible

Every phase takes as long as you need. During the trial lesson Diego estimates with you where you start.
