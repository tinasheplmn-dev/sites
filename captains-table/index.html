How does ordering online work?

Tap + on any dish, pick a collection time and leave your name and number. We start cooking and you collect at 16 Market Way. No account and no app fees.

How do I pay?

In cash when you collect. There is no online payment.

Is the food halal?

Yes. The Captain's Table is a halal Turkish fish and chip shop.

Do you deliver?

Not at the moment. Order here and collect it hot from 16 Market Way.

Can I still walk in and order at the counter?

Of course. Walk in Monday to Saturday between 11:00 and 21:00. It can get busy, so ordering ahead saves you the wait.

I have an allergy. Can you help?

Yes. Call us on 020 7093 3206 before you order and we tell you what goes into each dish.
