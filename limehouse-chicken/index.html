Questions or a big order?

Planning a party or ordering for the whole office? Send us a message. Want to order now? Use the menu, it's quicker.

The shop gets back to you by phone or email.

Can I just walk in and order?

Of course. Order at the counter and eat in or take it away. Ordering online just means your food is ready when you arrive.

How long does collection take?

Usually about 15 minutes. You can also pick a later time when you order online.

Do you deliver?

Yes, locally around Limehouse. When you order delivery, we call you to confirm your address and any delivery charge before we start cooking.

How do I pay?

At the counter when you collect, or at the door when we deliver. There is no online payment.

How hot is the Naga sauce?

Seriously hot. Both of our house sauces are made in our kitchen. New to it? Start with the chilli sauce. Any extra sauce is £0.25.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes: the Bean & Veggie Burger meal, the Veggie Wrap meal and our chips.
