Frequently asked

Quick answers

Is really everything halal?

Yes. The entire menu is 100% halal, from the steaks to the sauces. If you still have a question about a specific dish, just ask the team.

Do I need to book?

Walking in works whenever there is space, but on Fridays and weekends booking is wise. Online you can see right away which times are still free.

Can I come with a large group?

Of course. Up to 8 people you book directly online. For larger groups, send us a message through the form above and we confirm personally.

How do I choose how my steak is cooked?

You simply do that at the table: rare, medium rare, medium or well done. The grill master cooks it exactly that way.

Can I order for collection or delivery?

Yes, on the order page you pick your own time. You pay on collection or at the door. Ordering at the counter also works, of course.

Is there anything for children?

Yes: the children's menu El Chiquitto (€ 15.50) with a choice of steak, chicken, sausage, frikandel or sparerib, with fries, salad and an ice cream to finish.

Do you accept gift cards?

Only our own Rancho Bravo gift cards. Unfortunately we cannot accept other gift cards.
