How does ordering online work?

Tap + on anything on the menu, pick a collection time and leave your name and number. The kitchen cooks it fresh and you collect it at 641 Commercial Road. No account and no app fees.

How do I pay?

In cash at the counter when you collect. There is no online payment.

Can I still just walk in?

Of course. Eat in or take away, Monday to Friday 07:30-16:00 and Saturday 07:30-14:00. Ordering ahead just means your food is ready when you arrive.

Do you deliver?

No, Frank's Cafe is collection only. Order here and pick it up hot from the counter.

Is there a vegetarian breakfast?

Yes. Next to the Full English there is a vegetarian breakfast, plus plenty on toast, toasties and sides without meat.

I have an allergy. Can you help?

Yes. Call 020 7790 9009 before you order and the team will tell you what goes into each dish.
