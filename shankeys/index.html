Sacha & Eoghan

Born in an old bookies

Chef Sacha Henry cooks from Indian family recipes with Irish produce. Eoghan Shankey, Irish and trained behind the bar at Milk & Honey, runs the room and the cocktails.

  1. The bookies

    221 Well Street started life as a betting shop. Our kitchen is the old bookies' office.

  2. Loafing

    Then a bakery café. We kept its sign above the window, so look for "Loafing" when you arrive.

  3. Shankeys

    A pop-up in 2022 that never left. Six or seven tables, candles, cookbooks and floral china off grandma's dresser.

Do I need to book?

It's a small room with a handful of tables, so booking is the safest way to get a seat, especially on Friday and Saturday. Book online for 1 to 8 people.

How does the menu work?

Small plates made for sharing, plus a couple of larger plates for the table. Most tables order three or four small plates for two people, then a sharing plate.

Can you cater for vegetarians?

Yes. Several plates are vegetarian, such as the cauli cheese, buttered spuds and saag burrata. Tell us about allergies when you book.

Can we book the whole place?

Yes, for birthdays, team dinners and other celebrations. Send us a message with your date and group size and we come back to you personally.
