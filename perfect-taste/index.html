Good to know

Questions, answered

How does ordering online work?

Tap + next to any dish, choose collection or delivery and a time, and leave your name and number. You pay when you collect or when your order arrives. No account, no app fees.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Choose delivery at checkout and fill in your address. The shop confirms by phone whether your address is in range before your food goes on.

Can I eat in?

Yes. Plenty of guests sit in to eat. Walk in, order at the counter and take a seat.

Can you cater for a party or a big order?

Yes. Send a catering request with the date, the number of people and what you have in mind, and the team calls you back to put it together.

I have an allergy. Can you help?

Yes. Call 020 7538 1161 before you order and the team tells you exactly what goes into each dish.
