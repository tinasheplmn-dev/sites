FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I just walk in?

Yes, always. Order at the counter and eat in or take it away. Ordering ahead online is an extra: your order is ready at the time you choose.

What are the opening hours?

We’re open every day: Monday to Friday from 11.30 to 20.30, Saturday and Sunday from 16.30 to 20.30. Times may differ on public holidays and during holiday periods.

Do I need to book to eat in?

No booking needed. Walk in and grab a seat. There’s free WiFi and the place is wheelchair accessible.

Do you deliver?

Through this site you order to take away or to eat in. Your fries go straight from the fryer into the bag, and you skip the platform surcharge.

Are there vegetarian and halal options?

Absolutely. The menu has its own vegetarian category, all chicken products are 100% halal and everything is fried in plant-based, gluten-free oil.

How can I pay?

In store, by card or in cash. Online orders are paid at the counter too.

I have an allergy. What should I know?

We fry in plant-based, gluten-free oil and there’s a gluten-free frikandel. Mention your allergy at the counter or add it to the comments field of your online order. We can never fully rule out traces of allergens.

Freshly cut fries, 100% beef burgers and soft serve at the Onderwijsboulevard in 's-Hertogenbosch. Cafetaria 2.0: quality without the fuss.
