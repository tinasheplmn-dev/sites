FAQ

Good to know

Can I just walk in?

Yes, always. Order at the counter and eat in or take it away. Ordering online is handy when you don’t want to wait: your order is ready at the time you choose.

Can I pay online?

No, you pay at the counter: by card or in cash. That keeps it simple.

Do you deliver?

Through this site you order to take away or to eat in. Your fries go straight from the fryer into the bag, and you skip the platform surcharge.

I have an allergy. What should I know?

We fry in plant-based, gluten-free oil and there’s a gluten-free frikandel. Add your allergy to the comments field or mention it at the counter and we’ll help you out.
