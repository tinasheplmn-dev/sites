Brand story

Every gym deserves a title shot. Take yours.

Martial arts change lives. Discipline, confidence, a straight back. That is what you give people. But you never started your own gym to get buried in marketing and admin. You started for the mat, not for the mailbox.

Titleshot floors the guesswork, with an approach that actually works. No tools from people who have never seen a mat up close. No generic agency with smooth talk and little action. We are the cornerman of your gym. You are in the ring, we make sure you go home with the win.

The business side? We handle it with the same mindset you train with: a fighter’s. We go to the final round and are only satisfied when there is just one gym that counts in your city.

We turn gyms into title contenders. And contenders into undisputed champions. We deliver loyal members and build the machine that brings them in: a knockout-level website, marketing that fills your classes and systems that win back time. Your hands are free again and your gym becomes ‘the gym to beat.’

And the competition? They throw in the towel.
