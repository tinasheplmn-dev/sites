04 · 3D mail marketing

Mailbox Main Event

From €297per campaign

Every champion deserves a walkout nobody forgets.

Online, everyone fights for the same attention. The same posts, the same ads, the same message. Mailbox Main Event cuts through that noise. Not reach for the sake of reach, but one impression your future member cannot ignore. That is how your gym is not just seen, but remembered.

Why it works

  • The letterbox is almost empty. Online you compete with hundreds of ads, in the letterbox you are the only one.
  • A parcel always gets opened. Curiosity beats a flyer that goes straight into the recycling.
  • It stays around and gets passed on. People show it to their partner, neighbours or colleagues. That is free extra reach.
  • It feels personal. You address one person, not the masses, and that is how people respond.
  • It shows right away that your gym is different from the rest.

What is included

  • Audience and mailing list: together we decide who gets it: families nearby, former members, companies or schools. The more targeted the list, the more each parcel delivers.
  • Concept, object and letter: object, copy and offer tell one story together. It sticks and does not feel like a gimmick.
  • A strong offer: every recipient gets a reason to respond now, like a personal trial class or invitation.
  • Production and shipping: we take care of everything, from design to handwritten address.
  • Dedicated landing page and follow-up: recipients land on a dedicated page through a QR code and are followed up. More touchpoints mean more responses.
  • Measurable return: you see exactly how many sign-ups and how much revenue each campaign brings in.

06 · More from your member base

Member Multiplier Method

From €297per programme

The biggest opportunity lies in what you already have.

Former members who quit. Enquiries that were never followed up. Happy members nobody asks for an introduction. That is where almost every gym leaves money on the table. Member Multiplier Method gets the most out of your existing member base, without a single euro of advertising. Growth does not start outside your door, but often inside your gym.

Why it works

  • Convincing someone who already knows you is far cheaper than a stranger.
  • Happy members are your best salespeople, if you give them the chance.

What is included

  • Win-back: former members get a personal invitation to come back. Revenue you had already lost comes back.
  • Reactivating old enquiries: people who were once interested but never started get a new chance. New members from your own list, without ad costs.
  • Referral programme: members are rewarded when they bring someone in. Word of mouth becomes a steady source of new members instead of chance.
  • Review machine: more positive reviews, catching member feedback in time and higher satisfaction among your members. That makes new members choose you sooner and your members stay longer.
