Camp terms

The rules of the ticket

What you get, what we ask, and what is still being confirmed.

The camp

Maximum Impact Makers Youth Camp 2027 (MIMYC'27) is organised by The Church of Pentecost Netherlands and takes place from Wednesday 14 to Saturday 17 July 2027 at De Hoof, De Hoof 18, 5712 LM Someren, Netherlands.

The full-camp ticket

  • Price: EUR 149.99 per person.
  • Included: camp participation and activities, accommodation at De Hoof and all meals during camp.
  • Transport is not part of the ticket description until arrangements are confirmed. We do not describe it as included or excluded before then.
  • Day tickets and instalment options are not offered until leadership confirms them.

Registration and payment

  • Registering on this website records your details and gives you a registration number. It is not a confirmed place.
  • Payment is handled by the payment provider that leadership selects. Instructions follow by email. No payment is taken on this website.
  • Your place is confirmed after payment is received, and for attendees under 18 also after guardian permission is verified.
  • Capacity, the registration deadline and waitlist rules are confirmed before registration opens.

Cancellation and refunds

To be confirmed by leadership. The refund, cancellation and transfer rules are published here before registration opens.

Under 18

  • An attendee who is under 18 on 14 July 2027 needs permission from a parent or legal guardian.
  • Permission is given through the secure link sent after registration, or through an evidence route that the camp team can verify.
  • A youth leader may help but cannot give permission on a guardian's behalf. Payment does not count as permission.
  • Without verified permission, an attendee under 18 is not cleared for check-in.

Photos and video

Consent to appear in photos and video is optional and separate from attendance. You, or your guardian if you are under 18, can withdraw it at any time.

Code of conduct

The camp code of conduct and the safeguarding contact are published here before registration opens.
