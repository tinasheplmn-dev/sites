Privacy notice

How we handle your data

Short, in plain language, and only what registration for MIMYC'27 needs.

Who is responsible

The data controller for MIMYC'27 registrations is Stichting The Church of Pentecost Holland (The Church of Pentecost Netherlands). The official contact route for privacy questions is published here before registration opens.

What we collect, and why

  • Attendee details: name, email, mobile number, country and city, and for COP attendees the district and local assembly. We need this to register you, to send payment instructions and to reach you about camp.
  • Age indication: whether you are under 18 on 14 July 2027. We need this to know whether guardian permission is required.
  • Parent or guardian details (under 18 only): name, relationship, email and mobile number, plus an optional emergency contact. We need this to collect and verify permission and to reach someone in an emergency.
  • Permission record: who confirmed permission, when, and the optional photo and video consent. Attendance permission and media consent are stored separately.
  • Interest list: first name, email and attendee type, only if you ask to hear when registration opens.

We do not ask for medical, dietary or religious details during registration. If the camp team needs something like that, they ask you directly and store it separately with restricted access.

Who can see it

  • Only authorised members of the camp and safeguarding team can open registrations and permission records.
  • Local youth leaders only see the cases assigned to them, and never the uploaded or submitted permission details of others.
  • Payment is handled by the payment provider that leadership selects. That provider's own privacy notice applies to the payment itself.

How long we keep it

Registration and permission records are kept for the camp and the administrative follow-up afterwards, then deleted. The exact retention period is confirmed before registration opens and published here.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected, or withdraw your registration. A parent or guardian can withdraw permission at any time before camp. Media consent can be withdrawn at any time, separately from attendance.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no tracking cookies and runs no advertising scripts. If analytics are added later, this notice is updated first.
