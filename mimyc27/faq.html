When and where is camp?

MIMYC'27 takes place from Wednesday 14 to Saturday 17 July 2027 at De Hoof, De Hoof 18, 5712 LM Someren, Netherlands.

How much is the full-camp ticket?

The full-camp ticket is EUR 149.99 per person.

Are food and accommodation included?

Yes. Meals, accommodation and camp participation are included in the full-camp ticket.

Who can attend?

The camp is aimed at young people, particularly COP youth across the Netherlands. Guests and non-members are welcome, including from nearby countries. Exact age limits will be shared once confirmed.

Is transport provided?

Transport arrangements are still being finalised. We will share the details when confirmed.

How do I register?

Once registration is open, the Register button takes you through our approved booking process. Until then you can leave your details to be the first to hear.

I am under 18. Can I come?

Yes, as long as a parent or guardian gives permission. During registration you add their details, and they confirm through a secure link. A booking for someone under 18 is only cleared once that permission is verified. Exact age limits will be shared once confirmed.
