Why MIM

Grow in faith. Leave with purpose.

Maximum Impact Makers Youth Camp 2027 invites young people to grow in faith and consider the difference they can make in their churches and communities. Come ready to connect, reflect and be equipped for purposeful living.

Grow

Deepen your walk with Christ. Teaching and worship that go further than a Sunday service, and quiet moments that are yours.

What to expect

Teaching and worship sessions every day, and room for prayer and reflection. The full programme follows once confirmed.

Connect

Build meaningful friendships. Four days with young people from across the Netherlands and nearby countries, in one place.

What to expect

Meals, accommodation and activities together on one site at De Hoof. COP youth and guests, side by side.

Make impact

Take your faith and purpose beyond camp. Go home with a clear next step for your church, your city and your own life.

What to expect

Sessions about your purpose in your church and community, and time to decide on a next step to take home.

All questions and contact

When and where is camp?

MIMYC'27 takes place from Wednesday 14 to Saturday 17 July 2027 at De Hoof, De Hoof 18, 5712 LM Someren, Netherlands.

How much is the full-camp ticket?

The full-camp ticket is EUR 149.99 per person.

Are food and accommodation included?

Yes. Meals, accommodation and camp participation are included in the full-camp ticket.

Who can attend?

The camp is aimed at young people, particularly COP youth across the Netherlands. Guests and non-members are welcome, including from nearby countries. Exact age limits will be shared once confirmed.

Is transport provided?

Transport arrangements are still being finalised. We will share the details when confirmed.

How do I register?

Once registration is open, the Register button takes you through our approved booking process. Until then you can leave your details to be the first to hear.
