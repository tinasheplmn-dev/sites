Terrace & Bolwerk

A deck on the water, a rooftop park above it

Five centuries of fortification history under your feet, sun on your face, and boats drifting past.

The deck

You settle in here without a reservation

Bolwerk's terrace sits on a wooden deck built over the water. We deliberately don't take table reservations here: whoever arrives claims the best spot available.

  • Sun on the deck all afternoon
  • Lounge cushions on the jetty, feet above the water
  • Parasols and a blanket when it turns cooler
The jetty

Moor up, board, or just watch

Our own jetty sees a steady stream of sloops and canal tour boats coming and going. Arrive by water and you can simply moor with us; several canal tours through the old town use the jetty as a boarding and drop-off point.

A loop around the Binnendieze or the singels first, then drinks on the deck: an afternoon in Den Bosch doesn't get much better.

The story

Five centuries on watch by the water

14th century

The Koepoort, the gate leading out towards Vlijmen, stands here in the city wall.

1528

Because a gate is vulnerable, Bolwerk Sint-Jan rises: a triangular fortification with the new Sint Janspoort gate.

19th century

The fortification is largely demolished for faster drainage; its remains disappear underground.

2015

The current pavilion opens on the old foundations: a corten-steel building that sinks into the city rampart, designed by Van Roosmalen van Gessel Architecten, with a rooftop park by design studio OSLO.

Now

Inside, you dine next to the original medieval city wall; the information centre on the Den Bosch fortifications is next door.

Inside

Bright, spacious, and a wall from 1528

The many windows give you a view of the water from every seat inside. The large wooden reading table invites you to sit with a paper, a coffee and a real Bossche bol; the lounge corner is there for the slow Sunday afternoon.

  • The original city wall stands right in the middle of the restaurant
  • Generous reading table with newspapers and magazines
  • Wheelchair friendly and child friendly

Above your head

There's a park on our roof

The building sinks into the city rampart and carries a public rooftop park with trees and seating edges. Walk up the stairs after lunch: you'll look out over the singel and the city's old fortifications.

The Bolwerk Sint-Jan information centre tells the story of the Den Bosch fortifications there.
