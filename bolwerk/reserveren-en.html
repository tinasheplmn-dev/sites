Good to know

How booking works at Bolwerk

  • The terrace is always walk-in only, no reservations there
  • Between 11:30 and 15:30 we only take bookings for lunch
  • From 9 people, it goes through the group request form
  • You get an instant confirmation with a calendar file

Is your time slot full? Join the waiting list: as soon as a table frees up, we'll call you right away.
