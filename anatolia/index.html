What comes with a main?

Our grill mains come with rice, salad and bread. Kebabs with yoghurt or sauce come with rice and salad.

Do you have vegetarian dishes?

Yes. Falafel, moussaka, vegetarian grilled shish, vegetarian pide, a vegetarian stew and most of our cold meze are vegetarian. See the vegetarian dishes.

Do you deliver?

Yes, around Hackney. Order online, choose delivery and pay when your food arrives. You can also collect it yourself.

Can I book for a big group?

Yes. Book online for up to 8 people. For bigger groups and family gatherings, send us a message and we confirm personally.
