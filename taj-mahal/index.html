Good to know

Is the meat halal?

Yes, all our meat is halal.

How hot is hot?

Biryani, curries and doner come mild, medium or hot: you choose when you order. Naga wings are our hottest wings.

Do you deliver?

Yes, nearby. Choose delivery at checkout and we'll call you to confirm the delivery fee. Collection is always possible.

How do I pay?

You pay when you collect, or at the door when we deliver. No card details online and no app fees.

Do you cater for allergies?

Let us know in your order note or call us on 020 7265 9111 before you order, and we'll talk you through the menu.
