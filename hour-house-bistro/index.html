Good to know

Is everything halal?

Yes. Hour House is 100% halal and Muslim-owned, and we don't serve alcohol.

Can I really get a roast on a weekday?

Yes. The full roast menu runs every day from opening until close, alongside brunch.

Do you deliver?

Yes, nearby. Choose delivery at checkout and we'll call you to confirm the delivery fee. Collection is always possible.

Do I need to book?

Walk-ins are welcome. Weekends get busy around the roast, so booking a table is the safest bet.

Do you cater for allergies?

Tell us when you order or book. We do our best, but we can't guarantee allergen-free food because ingredients are handled in one kitchen.
