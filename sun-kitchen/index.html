Our story

The sun always rises

Three decades on Hackney's streets, one family, and the same heart under a new name.

  1. 1995

    A small Turkish restaurant

    We open our first place in Hackney: the flavours of home, a table for families, friends and neighbours.

  2. 2003

    TAD Restaurant

    A new chapter at 261 Mare Street. For 17 years, generations dine and make memories here.

  3. 2020

    The long night

    We have to close our doors, and turn a small storage space into a humble gozleme shop to keep cooking.

  4. 2024

    Sun Kitchen

    Back at our table on Mare Street. Still TAD at heart, now named after the sun that always rises.

“No matter how long the night, the sun always rises. Welcome to Sun Kitchen. Welcome home.”

Good to know

Is the meat halal?

Yes, our meat is halal.

Do you have vegetarian and vegan dishes?

Plenty. Look for V and VG on the menu: falafel, imam bayildi, vegetarian kebab, musakka, most cold meze and our salads.

Can I book for a big group?

Yes. Book up to eight people online. For nine or more, parties, anniversaries or meetings, send us a message or call 020 8510 0606.

Do you deliver?

Yes, nearby. Choose delivery at checkout and we'll call you to confirm the delivery fee. Collection from Mare Street is always possible.

Do you cater for allergies?

Before ordering, please speak to our staff about your requirements, or call us on 020 8510 0606. You can also add a note to your order.
