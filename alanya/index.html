Is everything halal?

Yes. All our meat is halal and we serve no alcohol. Soft nasheeds play in the restaurant instead of loud music.

Do you deliver?

Yes, around Bow and Roman Road. Order online, choose delivery and pay when your food arrives. You can also collect it yourself.

Can I book for a big group or iftar?

Yes. Book online for up to 8 people. For bigger groups, iftar and celebrations, send us a message and we confirm personally.

Is the restaurant family friendly?

Yes. We have high chairs, kids meals and step-free access for wheelchairs and buggies.
