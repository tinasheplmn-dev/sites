FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I order online at Sang Lee?

Yes. Via the "Order online" button you pick by number from the full menu, choose pickup or delivery and simply pay on receipt, by card or in cash.

Do you deliver to my address?

Enter your address with your order. If you're outside our delivery area, we'll give you a quick call to work something out.

Can I just walk in?

Of course. Walk in at Orthenstraat 57 and order at the counter. Ordering online is simply handy if you don't want to wait: your food is ready at the time you choose.

Can I eat in?

No, Sang Lee is a true takeaway without seating. You pick up or get it delivered, and enjoy at home.

Do you have vegetarian dishes?

Yes: pak choi, yardlong beans, tjap tjoy, gado gado and foe yong hai, all also available with fried tofu.

Do you cater?

Yes, we're happy to think along for larger groups. Send your request via the form above and we'll get in touch.

What are the opening hours?

Tuesday to Sunday from 12.00 to 20.00. We're closed on Mondays.

I have an allergy. Where can I mention it?

Add it to the comments field of your order, or give us a quick call. The kitchen is happy to help.
