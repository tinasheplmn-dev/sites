Since 1981

Two kitchens, one address on the Orthenstraat

At Sang Lee the Chinese-Indonesian and Surinamese kitchens have come together for 45 years. Regulars arrive in generations: for moksi meti with moksi sauce, roasted pork belly from the grill and yardlong beans just like in Paramaribo.

Everything is wokked fresh to order. And here you simply order by number, the way you always did.
