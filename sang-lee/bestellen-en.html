FAQ

Good to know

Can I just walk in?

Of course. Walk in at Orthenstraat 57 and order at the counter. Ordering online is simply handy if you don't want to wait: your food is ready at the time you choose.

Can I pay online?

No, you pay on receipt: by card or in cash, at the counter or at the door. That keeps it simple.

Do you deliver to my address?

Enter your address with your order. If you're outside our delivery area, we'll give you a quick call to work something out.

How do the choice menus and the rice swap work?

Add your choices (which dishes, and chaw fan, chaw min or mihoen instead of white rice) to the comments field. The kitchen simply adds any surcharge when you pay.

I have an allergy. Can the kitchen take it into account?

Add it to the comments field of your order and the kitchen will think along. In doubt, call us on 073 690 18 10.
