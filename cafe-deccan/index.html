Questions, groups or catering?

Want to eat now? Order online or book a table, that's quicker. For everything else, send us a message.

We get back to you by phone or email.

Can I just walk in?

Of course. Walk in for a table or order at the counter. Booking or ordering online just means your table or your food is ready when you arrive.

Is your food halal?

Yes, our kitchen is 100% halal.

Can you make it milder or spicier?

Yes. Tell us how hot you like it, in person or in the note when you order online, and the kitchen adjusts it.

Do you have vegetarian dishes?

Plenty. Look for the green Veg label on the menu: dosa, idli, vada, veg curries, veg biryani and more.

How quickly is a collection order ready?

Usually in about 20 minutes. You can also choose a later time, for example for your lunch break.

I have a food allergy. What should I do?

Every dish on the menu lists its allergens. Tell us about your allergy before you order. Nuts, gluten, dairy, mustard and soya are used in our kitchen, so we cannot guarantee a dish is free from traces.
