Good to know

How pickup works at Bagdad

Do you deliver?

No, Bagdad does not deliver. You collect your order at Orthenstraat 36; it will be ready at the time you choose, so there is no waiting.

Do I have to order online, or can I walk in?

Walking in is always fine. Ordering online has one big advantage: your order is ready at the time you choose, without waiting.

How do I pay?

On collection, by card or in cash. You do not pay anything online.
