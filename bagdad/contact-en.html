Frequently asked

Everything guests ask us

Can I book without calling?

Yes, via the "Book a table" button on every page. Pick a day and time, see availability instantly and get an immediate confirmation.

How large can my group be?

Online you can book up to 8 people. For larger groups, up to 36, send a message via the form; we reply within one working day.

Is the food halal?

We use halal meat. If you have specific questions about a dish, just ask: we will check for you and show the packaging where needed.

I have an allergy. Can you accommodate that?

Yes. Add your allergy to the notes field of your booking or order, or ask in the restaurant: we are happy to help and show you what goes into a dish.

Is there much vegetarian food?

Yes: homemade falafel, nearly all mezze, vegetarian Dolma, vegetarian Bryani, Tapsi Bagdad and more. Look for the veg label on the menu.

Where do I park?

The Wolvenhoek and Sint-Jan car parks are a few minutes on foot. The restaurant is five minutes from the Markt square.

Do you deliver?

No, we do not deliver. You can order online for pickup: your order is ready at the time you choose.

Dinner at Bagdad tonight?

صحتين sahtain: bon appetit, as we say in Iraq
