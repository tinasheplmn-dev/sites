From the market to Orthenstraat

It began between the stalls of the Den Bosch market

  • Years on the Den Bosch market

    Ahmed and his wife sold Iraqi dishes, falafel and mezze there. Den Bosch got to know their cooking.

  • A restaurant of their own

    The regulars asked for a place to sit down. So they opened their own restaurant on Orthenstraat.

  • Today

    Iraqi cuisine at the table every evening, and you will still find them at the market for a falafel sandwich.
