Stoofstraat 10

A window on Italy

Whoever walks through the narrow Stoofstraat looks straight into Italy. Our windows carry large sepia photographs: a canal in Venice, the balcony of Verona, an Italian-American pizzeria from the old days. Behind that glass we have been feeding Den Bosch for years.

Inside it is small and warm, and in summer the tables move out under the wisteria. It is the kind of place where a table for two and a family of five sit next to each other without anyone minding.

The kitchen

Pizza first, and then some

The card keeps the promise under our name. First 32 pizzas, thin and generously topped. And then the più: pasta in every direction, calzone, dishes from the oven, dolci and a coffee with a story.

  • Every pasta with your choice of spaghetti, tagliatelle or penne
  • Vegetarian? Look for the vegetariana, the cheese pizzas and the salads
  • Allergy or a change to a dish? Ask us, the kitchen thinks along
