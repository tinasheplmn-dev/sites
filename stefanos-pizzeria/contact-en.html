FAQ

Frequently asked

Do I need to book a table?

Up to 4 people: no, just walk in. Coming with 5 or more? Send us a message via the form above with the date, time and number of guests, and we will set a table.

What are your opening hours?

We are open daily from 4.30 pm: Sunday to Wednesday until 9 pm, Thursday to Saturday until 9.30 pm. On public holidays the hours may differ.

Can I order for pick-up or delivery?

Yes. Order online via the Order online button: you choose pick-up or delivery and a time that suits you. Delivery is free, with no minimum order.

How do I pay?

On receipt: by card or in cash, at the counter or at the door. There is no online payment.

Do you have vegetarian dishes?

Yes: think of the pizza and pasta vegetariana, the cheese pizzas, several salads and the lasagne vegetariana. See the menu for all options.

I have an allergy. What should I do?

Mention it in the note field of your order, or ask your question via the form above. The kitchen is happy to think along.

Where can I park?

The Stoofstraat itself is pedestrian territory. The car parks of the city centre, such as Sint-Josephstraat and Wolvenhoek, are a few minutes on foot.

The Stoofstraat pizzeria Den Bosch keeps coming back to: pizza, pasta e più, right in the city centre. Walk in, pick up or have it delivered.
