FAQ

Good to know

Can I pay online?

No, you pay on receipt: by card or in cash, at the counter or at the door. Simple for everyone.

Do you deliver to my address?

Delivery is free, with no minimum order. Enter your address with your order; if you are outside our delivery area, we will give you a quick call to sort something out.

I have an allergy. Can the kitchen take it into account?

Put it in the note field with your order and the kitchen will think along. In doubt? Ask your question via the contact page first.
