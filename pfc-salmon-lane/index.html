Good to know

How does ordering online work?

Tap + on anything on the menu, choose your drink or spice level, then pick collection or delivery and a time. Leave your name and number and you get a ticket number straight away. No account, no app fees.

Do you deliver?

Yes, to E14, E3 and E1 postcodes. Choose delivery when you order. The delivery fee and the minimum order show in your basket before you send it, and you pay when your order arrives.

Can I just walk in?

Of course. Walk in at 106 Salmon Lane and order at the counter, every day from 11:30 to 22:00. Ordering online just means your food is ready when you arrive.

How long does it take?

Collection orders are usually ready in about 15 minutes. Delivery takes about 35 minutes. You can also pick a later time.

Do meal deals come with a drink?

Most meal deals come with a can or bottle of your choice: Pepsi, Diet Coke, 7up, Orange Miranda, Capri Sun or still water. The Family Bucket comes with a 1.5 litre drink.

I have an allergy. Can you help?

Yes. Call us on 020 7538 1141 before you order and we tell you what goes into each item.
