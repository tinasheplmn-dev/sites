Our story

Since 1982, beneath the tower of St. John's

Rhodos was the first Greek restaurant in 's-Hertogenbosch, and more than forty years later it's still exactly the same place. Follow the line from then to now.

1982

The city's first Greek

Rhodos opens on the Kerkstraat, as the first Greek restaurant in 's-Hertogenbosch.

From Greece

Furniture and paintings

The owner had them shipped over directly. They still hang and stand there today.

The building

Three floors high

From a table for two to a party of seventy guests, with a view over the Kerkstraat.

Today

Dimitris and Giorgos

They welcome you at the door. In good weather you sit outside, in the shadow of the cathedral.
