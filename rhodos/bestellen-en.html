Good to know

Questions about ordering

Can I pay online?

No, you pay when you collect: by card or cash, at the bar. The 10% takeaway discount is already included in your total.

Do I have to order online, or can I just walk in?

Walking in is always fine. Ordering online has one advantage: your food is ready at the time you choose, with no waiting.

I have an allergy. Does the kitchen take that into account?

Add a note in the comments field when you order, and the kitchen will take it into account. If you're unsure about a dish, just ask at the bar when you collect.
