Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Do I need to book?

Especially at weekends and on summer evenings, that's a good idea: the restaurant is often full. The booking button shows you straight away which times are free and gives you instant confirmation.

Where can I park?

There are several car parks in the city centre. Cheaper option: park for free at a Transferium outside the centre; parking including return public transport tickets into the centre costs no more than € 4.00.

Is there a terrace?

In good weather you can sit outside on the Kerkstraat, with a view of St. John's Cathedral.

Can I come for lunch?

On Saturdays there's lunch from 11:30 to 15:00, with its own lunch menu. On other days we open at 15:30.

Can I get food to take away?

Yes, the whole menu is available to take away, with a 10% discount. Order online and choose your collection time, or just walk in and order at the bar. We don't deliver.

Can you take allergies into account?

Let us know about your allergy or dietary requirement when you book, or in the comments field of your order, and the kitchen will take it into account.

Do you also cater for large groups?

For groups of 10 to 70 guests there are two packages: the Choice menu (€ 33.50 pp) and the Greek Table (€ 36.00 pp). Enquire about a date.

Do you have work for me?

We regularly look for part-time staff for the bar, service and the dishes. Send us a message via the form above.
