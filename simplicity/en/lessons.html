06 Frequently asked questions

What students ask us before they start

From what age can I take driving lessons?

You can start lessons from age 16.5. You can take the practical exam from 17; until you turn 18 you then drive with a coach under the 2toDrive scheme.

How many lessons will I need?

On average a student in the Netherlands needs around 40 to 45 hours. It depends on your experience, feel for traffic and how often you take lessons. After the trial lesson we give you an honest estimate for your situation.

Do I need to pass my theory exam first?

No, you can start driving lessons before passing the theory exam. Do pass it in time though: without the theory certificate you cannot take the interim test or the practical exam.

I already have a foreign licence. Can I exchange it?

That depends on the country that issued it; some licences can be exchanged, others require the full Dutch exams. Check the RDW website for your situation. If you do need the Dutch exams, a refresher-style course usually gets you ready faster than a full beginner course.

How often can I take lessons each week?

That is up to you. Most students pick two lessons a week. In a hurry? We plan more, evenings and Saturdays included.

Can I take the CBR exams in English?

Yes. The CBR offers both the theory exam and the practical exam in English. We prepare you in the same language as your lessons.

What if I fail my exam?

We look at what went wrong, train those points specifically and book a re-exam (€300) as soon as you are ready. A BNOR exam or an exam-anxiety exam are also options.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, paying in instalments is possible by arrangement, so money does not have to stand between you and your licence.
