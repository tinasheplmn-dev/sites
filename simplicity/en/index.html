01 The approach

Quality first. That is exactly why you pass.

Many driving schools push you towards the exam as fast as possible. We turn it around: you first learn to drive really well and safely, and the exam becomes a logical consequence. That is why so many of our students pass on their first attempt.

  • Every lesson has a goalLessons build on each other, and your progress card shows exactly where you stand. No driving laps just to fill the hour.
  • An eye for detailObservation, mirrors, deciding at the right moment: Sartip lets nothing slide. Students literally call his strictness a strength in their reviews.
  • Calm in the carExplanations in different ways until it clicks, without rush and without raised voices. Also if driving makes you nervous.

06 How it works

From trial lesson to licence, without the noise

The trial lesson

60 minutes of driving for €50. You meet your instructor and get an honest assessment afterwards: how many lessons you need and which package fits. Nothing more, nothing less.

Your lesson plan

One fixed instructor, 90-minute lessons and pick-up at home or work in Eindhoven, Veldhoven, Best or Waalre. We plan around your agenda, several lessons a week if you want.

The exam

You authorise us via DigiD with school number 7354K8. We book your interim test and practical exam at the CBR in the Eindhoven region the moment you are ready, in English if you prefer.

Feel the difference in one trial lesson

60 minutes behind the wheel with Sartip or Gayal, followed by honest advice on what you need. No obligations, no small print.
