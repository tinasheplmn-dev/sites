04 The honest story

What will you spend in total?

On average a student in the Netherlands needs around 40 to 45 hours of lessons; a complete licence typically costs between €2,500 and €3,500 nationwide. Some need less, others more. That is why every route with us starts with a trial lesson: afterwards you know what it will be for you, before you commit to anything.
