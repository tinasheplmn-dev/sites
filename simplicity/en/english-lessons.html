01 Who it is for

Built for the Brainport region

Many of our students are here for work or study: from ASML to TU/e. They share the same challenge: learning Dutch traffic rules with little time next to a demanding job. That is exactly what our approach is built for.

  • Lessons fully in EnglishNo half-translated jargon: Sartip explains every traffic situation clearly in English, and switches to Dutch just as easily.
  • Flexible around your jobEvening and Saturday lessons, pick-up at work or at home. Reviews mention the flexible planning remarkably often.
  • Exams in English tooThe CBR offers both the theory exam and the practical exam in English. We prepare you in the same language.

03 Good to know

Dutch traffic, decoded

Bikes rule

Cyclists are everywhere in Eindhoven, and they often have priority. We train you until checking for bikes becomes a reflex, not a checklist item.

Signs and shark teeth

Priority roads, give-way markings and zone signs work differently than in many countries. You learn to read the road, not just the rules.

The CBR system

Theory exam, interim test, practical exam: we guide you through the Dutch system step by step and book everything for you at the right moment.

Start with one hour behind the wheel

A 60-minute trial lesson for €50, entirely in English. Afterwards you get an honest assessment of how many lessons you need. No obligations.
