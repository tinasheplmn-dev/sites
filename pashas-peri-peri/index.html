Good to know

Which heats can I choose?

Mild, lemon & herb, hot and extra hot on all peri peri chicken, wings, strips, wraps and burgers. The platters can also come jerk BBQ.

Do you deliver?

Yes, nearby. Choose delivery at checkout and we'll call you to confirm the delivery fee. Collection is always possible.

How do I pay?

You pay when you collect, or at the door when we deliver. No card details online and no app fees.

Do you cater for allergies?

Let us know in your order note or call 020 7790 8383 before you order, and we'll talk you through the menu.
