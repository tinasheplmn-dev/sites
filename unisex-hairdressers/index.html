Good to know

Before your visit

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome whenever we are open. Booking online simply holds a time and a stylist for you, so you skip the wait.

Do you cut men's and children's hair?

Yes. We are a unisex salon: women, men and children are all welcome, first haircuts included.

My colour has gone brassy or orange. Can you fix it?

Yes. Book a toning appointment, or walk in for advice. We tone brassy and orange hair back to the shade you wanted.

How do I pay?

You pay in the salon after your appointment. Booking online is free, with no deposit and no booking fees.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Of course. Call us on 020 7709 8338 with your booking number and we will move or cancel it.
