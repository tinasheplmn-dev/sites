Our story

The whole world pulls up a chair with us

Al Mundo means "to the world". That's exactly what happens every evening on the Orthenstraat.

Osman and Kamil, hosts through and through

Host Osman welcomes you as if you're eating at his own home, and son Kamil stands beside him in service. Guests mention them both by name in their reviews.

  • A menu around the Mediterranean, from Greek salad to Filetto alla Angelo
  • 3 courses for the price of 2 on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday
  • Vegetarian and marked gluten-free dishes on the same menu
  • Mains between € 17.50 and € 26.50, sides included

The building

The street above, the vaults below

Upstairs you eat facing the street, with doors opening onto the terrace when the weather allows. Downstairs you sit beneath the vaulted cellar, among candlelight and wicker platters.

  • Terrace on the Orthenstraat in fine weather
  • Vaulted cellar for an evening for two or with a group
  • In the city centre, just off the bustle
