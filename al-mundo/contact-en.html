Frequently asked questions

Can I book a table online?

Yes. Using the booking button you choose your day and time, and your reservation is confirmed straight away. You see immediately which times are still free.

When is Al Mundo open?

Tuesday to Sunday from 17.00 to 22.00. We are closed on Monday, except on public holidays.

Can I collect food?

Yes, Tuesday to Sunday between 16.00 and 20.00. Order online via the order page, and your order will be ready at the time you choose. We do not deliver.

What does an evening at Al Mundo cost?

Main courses cost between 17.50 and 26.50 euros, including chips, roast potatoes and fresh vegetables. On Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday you eat 3 courses for the price of 2, except on public holidays.

Are there gluten-free and vegetarian options?

Yes. The menu has marked gluten-free starters, mains and desserts, and vegetarian dishes such as the Vegetariana and Penne della casa.

Can I pay with a gift card?

Yes, we accept the Diner Card, VVV card and Dinercheque among others, per table.

Can I come with a group?

Yes. Up to and including 8 people you book directly online; for larger groups you send a message via the contact form above, and we'll confirm personally.
