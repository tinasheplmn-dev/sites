Good to know

Can I have my order delivered?

No, Al Mundo does not deliver. You collect your order at Orthenstraat 284; it will be ready at the time you choose, so there's no need to wait.

Do I have to order online, or can I just walk in?

You can always walk in. Ordering online has one big advantage: your order is ready at the time you choose, without waiting.

How do I pay?

On collection, by card or cash. You don't pay anything online.
